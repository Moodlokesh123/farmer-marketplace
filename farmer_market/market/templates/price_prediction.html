How AI Price Prediction Works

1

Historical Data

The system collects previous modal market prices for the selected crop.

2

Data Processing

Historical dates and prices are converted into numerical features for the machine learning model.

3

ML Model

A regression model learns the relationship between historical dates and modal prices.

4

Prediction

The trained model estimates the next expected modal market price.

ℹ️ Important Information

The prediction is an estimate generated from historical market-price records. Actual prices can vary because of demand, supply, weather, transportation, seasonality and other market conditions.

This prediction should be treated as a decision-support feature rather than a guaranteed future market price.
